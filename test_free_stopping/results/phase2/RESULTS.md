### Throughput (block 4)

32 short runs (8 seeds × 4 apps, 3k steps) per setting. Every setting bit-identical.

| Setting | Aggregate steps/s | Mean run (s) |
|---|---|---|
| 4 workers | 819 | 14.2 |
| 8 workers | 1212 | 18.5 |
| 12 workers | 1329 | 22.5 |
| 16 workers | 1378 | 29.1 |
| **MPS, 16 workers** | **2547** | 16.3 |

The Phase 1b collapse (24 steps/s at 32 workers) is gone: the thread-share and on-GPU
batch-order fixes work. Time-slicing saturates the GPU (98% utilization) around 16
workers; MPS, which lets kernels from different processes run concurrently, nearly
doubles throughput. The corpus runs under MPS.

### Learning-rate scan (block 2)

516 runs, 5k steps, 16 workers, 30 min. 21 diverged (all at large lr with large α).

- **Adam family:** every scanned lr (1e-4 to 3e-3) fits every variant at the middle α.
  The published lrs are kept: 3e-4 teacher–student, 1e-3 otherwise. Teacher–student with
  Adam+L2 reaches only ~0.6 train accuracy, a weight-decay effect (γ = 1e-4 caps how
  precisely it fits under the RMS < 0.01 criterion), not an lr effect.
- **MNIST depth 8, SGD family, α = 1 never trains.** Train accuracy stays at 0.12 at every
  lr; the loss stops at 0.09, the loss of predicting the mean one-hot target. With eight
  ReLU layers at default init the signal vanishes before the output. SGD α levels on
  depth 8 become {2, 4}.
- **SGD's usable lr depends strongly on α.** MNIST depth 8: up to 1.0 at α = 2, but only
  ≤ 3e-4 at α = 4 (a 1000× gap), hence one lr per α there. MNIST depth 3: ≤ 1.0 at α ≤ 2,
  ≤ 0.1 at α = 4.
- **Modular arithmetic with SGD** only trains at the top of the scanned range: plain SGD
  needs lr = 1 at α ≤ 2 (lr = 1 at α = 1 is just starting to fit at 5k steps). The true
  limit lies above the grid.
- **The first selection rule was too lenient.** It required only that the loss fall and
  not blow up, so it passed the depth-8 α = 1 runs and picked lr = 0.3 for modular SGD,
  which reaches ~0.3 train accuracy in 5k steps. `lr_select.classify` now also requires
  fitting (train accuracy ≥ 0.9, or the loss down to ≤ 5% of its start).
